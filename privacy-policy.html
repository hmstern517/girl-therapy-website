1. Who we are

Girl Therapy ("Girl Therapy," "we," "us," or "our") is a telehealth matching platform operated by Finally Therapy, LLC ("Company"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit girltherapy.co (the "Site"), join our waitlist, text our SMS program, or otherwise interact with our services (together, the "Services").

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at privacy@girltherapy.co.

2. Information we collect

a. Information you give us directly

  • Waitlist / contact info: phone number and/or email address, SMS consent status.
  • SMS "front desk" conversations: general questions you text us (e.g., about how the service works, scheduling, pricing). We ask that you not share health details, symptoms, or medical history over text — see our SMS Program Terms.
  • Therapist-matching intake (secure web form): information you provide through our secure intake form to help us match you with a therapist, which may include health-related information you choose to share.
  • Therapist applicant info: name, email, states licensed, license type, modalities, and related professional details, if you apply to join our therapist network.

b. Information collected automatically

  • Standard web analytics (e.g., pages visited, device/browser type, approximate location from IP) if analytics tooling is enabled on the Site.
  • Message metadata from our SMS provider (e.g., timestamps, delivery status) necessary to operate the texting program.

c. Information we do NOT intentionally collect over SMS

Our general SMS "front desk" channel is designed to avoid collecting protected health information (PHI) or other sensitive personal data. If you send us sensitive health information by text anyway, see Section 7 below.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your questions and operate the waitlist and SMS program.
  • To match you with an appropriate therapist through our secure intake process.
  • To send you service-related updates (e.g., waitlist status, onboarding steps).
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the Services.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not use your information to make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects without human review, and we do not sell your personal information.

7. If you send us sensitive information by text

Our SMS program is not intended for sharing health details, symptoms, crisis disclosures, or other sensitive personal information. If you do so anyway:

  • Our team is notified to follow up with you directly and route you to the appropriate secure channel.
  • If your message indicates a safety emergency, we will direct you to call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or call 911. Girl Therapy is not a crisis service.
  • We limit how that message is logged and who can access it internally.
