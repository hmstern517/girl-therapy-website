Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2027  |  Last updated: November 1, 2027

1. Who we are

Girl Therapy ("we," "us," or "our") is operated by HJS Capital, doing business as Girl Therapy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information you share when you visit girltherapy.co (the "Site") or join our waitlist, including by text.

Girl Therapy is currently a pre-launch marketing waitlist. The Site and our SMS program exist only to let you sign up to hear about our launch.

Questions? Contact us at hi@girltherapy.co.

2. Information we collect

  • Waitlist info: your mobile number and SMS consent status when you join our waitlist.
  • Messages you send us: the content of texts you send (e.g., texting HI to join, or replying STOP/HELP).

3. SMS data — how we collect, use, and protect it

When you join our waitlist by texting HI to (844) 752-0675, or by opting in on our Site, we collect your mobile phone number and SMS opt-in status.

  • We use this information only to send you waitlist and launch-related updates, and to respond to messages you send us.
  • We do not sell or share your mobile number or SMS opt-in data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.
  • Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.
  • Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe, or HELP for help.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

4. How we share information

We share information only with our SMS/telecom provider, to deliver and receive text messages, and with service providers who help us operate the waitlist, under confidentiality obligations. We may also disclose information when required by law. We do not sell your personal information or share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Your choices

  • SMS opt-out: Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help.
  • Access / deletion requests: Contact hi@girltherapy.co to request access to, or deletion of, your personal information.

6. Children's privacy

Our Services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or as otherwise defined by applicable law).

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here with a new "Last updated" date.
