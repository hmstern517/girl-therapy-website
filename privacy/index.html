Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2027  |  Last updated: November 1, 2027

1. Who we are

Girl Therapy ("Girl Therapy," "we," "us," or "our") is a telehealth matching platform operated by HJS Capital, doing business as Girl Therapy ("Company"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit girltherapy.co (the "Site"), join our waitlist, text our SMS program, or otherwise interact with our services (together, the "Services").

Right now, Girl Therapy is in a pre-launch, waitlist stage. We are not providing therapy or medical services — this Site and our SMS program are simply how we market the upcoming service and sign people up to hear about launch. Nothing you share with us through the waitlist or SMS program is treated as a medical record.

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at hi@girltherapy.co.

2. Information we collect

a. Information you give us directly

  • Waitlist / contact info: your mobile number (and/or email address if you choose that path), and SMS consent status.
  • SMS conversations: the content and metadata of text messages you send us (e.g., when you text HI to join, ask questions, or reply STOP/HELP).

b. Information collected automatically

  • Standard web analytics (e.g., pages visited, device/browser type, approximate location from IP) if analytics tooling is enabled on the Site.
  • Message metadata from our SMS provider (e.g., timestamps, delivery status) necessary to operate the texting program.

3. SMS data — how we collect, use, and protect it

When you join our waitlist by text (texting HI to (844) 752-0675) or check the SMS consent box on our Site, we collect your mobile phone number, your SMS opt-in status, and the history of messages exchanged with our texting number.

  • We use this information only to send you waitlist and launch-related updates about Girl Therapy and to respond to messages you send us.
  • We do not sell or share your mobile number or SMS opt-in data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.
  • Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.
  • Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe, or HELP for help.
  • Supported carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

4. How we use your information

  • To operate the waitlist and SMS program, and to respond to your questions.
  • To send you service-related updates (e.g., waitlist status, launch announcements).
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the Services.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not use your information to make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects without human review, and we do not sell your personal information.

6. Not a therapy service, not medical records

Girl Therapy's Site and SMS program are, at this stage, solely a waitlist and marketing channel for a platform we are building. We are not currently providing therapy, medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and nothing you share with us through the waitlist or SMS program is treated as a medical record or protected health information.

7. Data retention

We retain waitlist and SMS information for as long as necessary to operate the waitlist and SMS program, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes. If you reply STOP, we retain a record of your opt-out to honor it, but stop sending further messages.

8. Your choices & rights

  • SMS opt-out: Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help.
  • Access / deletion requests: Contact hi@girltherapy.co to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable legal exceptions.
  • State privacy rights: Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) or Washington's My Health My Data Act.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and text messages in particular transit carrier networks we do not control.

10. Children's privacy

Our Services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or as otherwise defined by applicable law).

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here with a new "Last updated" date, and, where required, provide additional notice.
