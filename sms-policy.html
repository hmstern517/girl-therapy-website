Girl Therapy SMS Program Terms

Girl Therapy (operated by Finally Therapy, LLC) offers an SMS program that lets you ask questions, learn about our services, and begin the process of getting matched with a therapist — all by text.

How to join

Text HI to (844) 752-0675, or check the SMS consent box when joining our waitlist at girltherapy.co. By doing either, you agree to receive text messages from Girl Therapy at the mobile number provided.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on your conversation with us. You may receive multiple messages during an active conversation, and occasional follow-ups afterward.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Opt out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages from Girl Therapy. You'll receive one final confirmation text.

Help

Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us at support@girltherapy.co.

What we don't do over text

Please don't share health details, symptoms, or medical history over text — this channel is for general questions and getting started only. We'll always direct anything sensitive to a secure, private form.

Privacy

We don't sell or share your phone number with third parties for marketing purposes. A full Girl Therapy Privacy Policy is coming soon; in the meantime, questions can be directed to support@girltherapy.co.

Supported carriers include major U.S. wireless carriers. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Girl Therapy is not a crisis service — if you're in danger or crisis, call or text 988, or call 911.
